Verification Guidelines & Review Criteria

1. National ID (NIC): Must clearly show legal name, date of birth, photo, and official NIC number. (Required)
2. Live Selfie: Face must be unobscured and match the photo on your government identity card. (Required)
3. Police Report: Official certificate validating a clear criminal background to protect clients. (Required)
4. Educational / NVQ: Certified NVQ diploma or recognized trade qualification for your skill category. (Optional)
