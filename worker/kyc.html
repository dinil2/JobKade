Verification Guidelines & Rules

  • Mandatory Documents (Must): Both National Identity Card (NIC) and Police Clearance Report are required for account verification.
  • Optional Documents: Trade certifications, NVQ diplomas, and apprenticeship certificates are optional and boost your worker profile credibility.
  • Clear & Legible: Ensure all numbers, full legal name, and expiration dates are clearly readable.
  • Supported Formats: PDF documents, PNG, and JPG/JPEG image formats up to 5MB.
  • Review Timeframe: Administrators review submissions within 12 to 24 business hours.
  • Verified Badge Benefits: Verified workers receive up to 3.5x more customer messages & jobs and priority listing on the map.
