Verification Guidelines & Review Criteria

Administrators cross-check your 3 core records before awarding your verified professional badge:

  • 1. Live Verification Selfie (Mandatory): Clear photo holding your NIC next to your face. Administrators check that your facial features match the ID photograph.
  • 2. National Identity Card (NIC) (Mandatory): Front and back scan or photo of your official government NIC. Full legal name and NIC number must match.
  • 3. Police Clearance Report (Mandatory): Valid certificate issued by the Sri Lanka Police confirming a clear background for client trust.
  • 4. Trade Certifications (Optional): NVQ Diplomas, technical apprenticeship certificates, or vendor badges to boost ranking.
  • Fast Approvals: Administrators review all 3 documents within 12 to 24 business hours.
