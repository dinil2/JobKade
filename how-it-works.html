How JodKade Works

Find the right person for your job in three simple steps.

For Customers

Finding a trusted worker is fast and simple.

01

Search

Enter the service you need and your location to browse verified workers nearby.

02

Choose

Compare worker profiles, read reviews, check verified badges, and view their work portfolio.

03

Contact

Send direct in-app messages, call, or WhatsApp the worker to discuss details and schedule.

For Workers

Start getting customers for your skills.

01

Register

Create your worker profile with your service details and submit your NIC for identity verification.

02

Get Verified

JodKade administrators review your identity. Once approved, you receive a verified badge.

03

Get Jobs

Create your service listings and subscribe to access customer job requests.
